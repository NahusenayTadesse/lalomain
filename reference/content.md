## Page: About us (`/about`)

**Meta title:** About Lalo | Our Journey from Coffee Trading to Global Logistics

### Hero
Badge: **Est. Sep 2025**
**Heading:** About Lalo Import & Export
> A diversified trading group connecting premium Ethiopian products with global markets through trust, quality, and strong partnerships.

### Who We Are
Lalo Import and Export (Lalo General Trading) is a multi-sector business group operating across import, export, retail, and service industries. Rooted in Ethiopia, we serve both local and international markets.

We operate as a unified parent company overseeing specialized subsidiaries, each built to meet specific market needs while maintaining shared standards and values.

### Our Story
Founded in September 2025, Lalo Import and Export was inspired by the opportunity to share Ethiopia's exceptional coffee with the world while responsibly importing high-quality products that support everyday life and business growth.

### Mission & Vision

**Our Mission**
To deliver premium locally sourced coffee to global markets while importing reliable, high-quality goods that support innovation, growth, and community development.

**Our Vision**
To become a leading global exporter of Ethiopian coffee and a trusted import partner recognized for excellence, sustainability, and operational integrity.

### Our Core Values (full list — 8 values)

1. **Integrity** — We conduct all business with honesty, transparency, and ethical practices across our supply chain.
2. **Quality** — We are committed to delivering high-quality coffee and importing reliable, trusted products.
3. **Sustainability** — Supporting environmentally responsible farming, fair trade principles, and long-term ecological balance.
4. **Community Empowerment** — Uplifting local farmers, creating opportunities, and contributing to regional economic development.
5. **Partnership** — Building strong, respectful relationships with producers, suppliers, and international buyers.
6. **Innovation** — Continuously improving processes, logistics, and services to remain efficient and competitive.
7. **Accountability** — Taking responsibility for our actions and consistently delivering on our commitments.
8. **Customer Focus** — Prioritizing the needs of our clients and striving to exceed expectations in every service.

### How We Operate
Lalo operates through a structured group model, with each subsidiary focused on a specific sector while supported by centralized standards, governance, and logistics. This approach allows us to remain agile, maintain quality control, and deliver consistent experiences across all our businesses.
